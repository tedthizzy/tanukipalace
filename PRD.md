# Drunk Tanuki Palace - Product Requirements Document

## Overview
Drunk Tanuki Palace is a whimsical, single-player digital izakaya experience where users interact with jovial, sake-loving tanuki characters. The palace is a cozy, illustrated space celebrating Japanese folklore and the mischievous nature of tanuki.

## Problem Statement
Modern life lacks whimsy and connection to folklore traditions. Users need a playful escape where they can enjoy the company of legendary creatures known for their love of sake and good times.

## Target Audience
- Fans of Japanese folklore and mythology
- Users seeking lighthearted, relaxing digital experiences
- Collectors of virtual companions and characters

## Platform & Scope (Assumptions for Feasibility)
- Platform: Web (desktop + mobile), delivered as a lightweight, single-page app
- Visuals: 2D illustrated scenes with subtle animations (no free-roam 3D)
- Interaction: Point-and-click/tap interactions and short dialogue sequences
- Content: Scripted dialogue and stories (no AI generation at MVP)
- Session length: 5–10 minutes

## Core Features

### 1. The Palace
- A beautifully illustrated traditional Japanese izakaya setting
- Single explorable scene at MVP (main hall), with a light day/night ambience shift
- Future rooms (sake cellar, garden, rooftop) after MVP

### 2. Tanuki Characters
- 3–4 tanuki with distinct personalities at MVP
- Mood system: sober, tipsy, merry, plastered
- Scripted folklore stories and short “sake wisdom” lines
- Light shapeshifting antics as simple visual gags

### 3. Sake Collection
- 5–8 sake varieties at MVP
- Each sake affects mood and dialogue variations
- Seasonal and rare sake events post-MVP

### 4. Interactions
- Pour sake for tanuki to increase friendship
- Listen to tanuki songs and tales
- Witness legendary tanuki belly drum performances
- Trade items with shapeshifting merchants

## MVP Definition (Feasible First Release)
- 1 room (main hall) with day/night ambience swap
- 3–4 tanuki, each with 3–5 short story beats
- 5–8 sake items with simple mood effects
- 2–3 interaction loops (pour, story, performance)
- Basic persistence (friendship level and last served sake)

## Success Metrics
- Daily active tanuki interactions
- Sake bottles served per session
- 7-day return rate (lightweight retention)
- Belly drum performances witnessed

## Non-Goals (MVP)
- Multiplayer sessions
- AI-generated dialogue or storytelling
- Fully explorable 3D palace
- Deep crafting or economy systems

## Risks & Mitigations
- Content volume risk: keep MVP dialogue short and reusable; add content post-launch
- Art bandwidth risk: use 2D illustrated scenes and modular character poses
- Retention risk: add light progression via friendship tiers and unlockable stories

## Milestones (Rough)
- Week 1–2: Visual direction, core loop design, prototype UI
- Week 3–5: Art production for main hall + tanuki + animations
- Week 6–7: Implement interactions, mood system, persistence
- Week 8: QA, polish, launch prep

## Future Considerations
- Multiplayer izakaya sessions
- Tanuki customization options
- Seasonal festivals (Moon viewing, New Year's)
- Kitsune rival palace expansion
